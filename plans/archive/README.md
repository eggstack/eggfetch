# Planning Archive

Completed, superseded, or abandoned interim plans move here once they are no
longer active. Archive moves MUST preserve traceability and SHOULD retain
original filenames and subsystem grouping.

## Grandfathered legacy archive

The ~250 flat `plans/*.md` files written before the 2026-09-25 planning
migration are archive-in-place: frozen historical records that stay at their
original paths because qualification evidence, compatibility docs, and
profiles cite them by path. See `plans/README.md` § Legacy archive. They
MUST NOT be extended with new active scope and SHOULD NOT be moved.

## Going forward

New completions move under `plans/archive/<subsystem>/` (or
`plans/archive/legacy/` for any late-migrated flat file) once the subsystem
roadmap and `plans/registry.md` no longer reference them as active.

Canonical long-term documents (`plans/000`–`003`) and accepted ADRs MUST NOT
be archived merely because their initial implementation completed.
